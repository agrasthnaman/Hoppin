## Long description

Hoppin is one shared place for a group trip.

The people you approve can see the days, the map, the money, and the documents. Someone who is not in the trip cannot.

Days is the plan, one day at a time. The map shows the stops. Money is who paid and who owes whom. The amounts are notes the group types. Nothing is charged. Documents are tickets and other files for that trip.

A copy of the trip stays on the phone, so the plan still opens with no signal.

Posters are drawn on the phone. Photos picked for a poster stay on the phone and are not uploaded.

You sign in with your email and a password. You can delete the account from Account, then Delete my account. The privacy page says what that removes, and what stays with the group.
